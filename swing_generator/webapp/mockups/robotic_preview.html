SwingPulse · visual direction

Your app is
already half
machine.

The amber gave it away. #fbbf24 on true black is avionics amber — cockpit annunciators, CRT terminals, lab equipment. The palette is already an instrument panel. What still reads as a consumer app is the geometry, the lettering, and the motion: 12px bubbles, a humanist typeface on the numbers, and a heartbeat. A touch of robotic doesn't repaint anything. It changes those three.

Vocabulary

Six moves, no repaint

Every colour below is already in style.css. These are shape, type and timing rules — the parts of the design that say "a machine wrote this" without saying it in words.

GEOM

Chamfer, don't round

A 45° cut on two opposing corners reads instantly as panel. Uniform 12px radius reads as bubble. Split the difference by meaning: machine-authored surfaces get the cut, things a finger presses keep the radius.

TYPE

Mono for machine speech

Numbers, tickers, timestamps, state words and micro-labels move to ui-monospace — SF Mono on your devices, so no webfont, no extra kilobyte. Prose stays humanist. The split is the effect: the machine reports, the app explains.

STAT

LEDs, not glows

A soft coloured halo is a mood. A 7px square that is solid, breathing or hard-blinking is a readout — three discrete states a person learns once and reads forever. Pair it with a mono status word and the glow budget drops to near zero.

MOTN

Move in quanta

The single strongest signal, and the cheapest. Machines don't ease — they step. Swap cubic-bezier for steps(12) on counters and bars, and the same component reads as sampled rather than animated.

RAIL

Let it report on itself

One hairline strip under the topbar, mono and muted, stating what the machine currently is: dataset, timeframe, instrument count, last tick, link state. It's the strip at the top of this page. It also gives the stale-data banner a permanent home instead of a red interruption.

Dosage

How far to take it

"A touch" is tier one. The tiers escalate, and each one is shippable on its own — tier two only makes sense once tier one is live and has stopped feeling new.

01

Touch Recommended

  • Mono for all numerals, tickers, timestamps and micro-labels; prose untouched
  • Chamfer on panels and status chips; radius stays on buttons and nav
  • The system rail under the topbar, absorbing the stale-data banner
  • LED + status word replacing the coloured glow on signal state
  • steps() on counters and stat bars
Cost · style.css tokens + ~6 component rules · no data, no signal logic, no JS behaviour
02

Instrument

  • Heartbeat → scan cycle on Market Pulse, keeping the exact bullish/bearish/mixed mapping
  • Segmented bars everywhere a count is shown; sector strips become 12-cell readouts
  • Grid substrate and tick rails on panel edges
  • Loader becomes a boot sequence that names what it's loading — the honest version of a spinner
Cost · one animation rewrite + the bar component · a day of tuning on a real phone
03

Full HUD — not in the app

  • Scanlines, corner brackets on everything, terminal typewriter reveals, machine microcopy
  • On a tool you open before the market does, this becomes a costume you have to see through to read a number
  • Spend it on the share card instead, where the whole job is to look like something worth screenshotting
Cost · cheap to build, expensive to live with
Guardrails

What the direction must not touch

Robotic goes wrong in four predictable ways, and three of them would damage things this app already got right.

Hold

Semantic colour

Green is buy, red is sell, violet is volume, amber is the system's own voice. A cyan-everything HUD palette would flatten a distinction the app is built on. The direction adds no new hue.

Hold

The human sentences

"Sectors are moving on their own, not all together — a normal day to pick setups." That line is the reason the dashboard is trustworthy. Mono-caps it into SECTOR DISPERSION NOMINAL and you've traded comprehension for atmosphere.

Hold

Legibility on glass

No scanlines or grid over anything numeric. On an OLED phone in the morning they alias into the digits. The substrate stays behind panels, never across them.

Hold

Meaning in motion

Today's heartbeat is bound to market state. A scan cycle must keep that binding, and a stepped counter must land visibly still — a number that looks like it's settling is a number you don't trust.
