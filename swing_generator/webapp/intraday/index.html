Signal Types
P1 Trend Reversal — Price crosses entire MA ribbon. Max penetration: 4H 2% / D 1.5% / W 2.5% / M 3%
P2 200 MA Bounce — Price pulls back to 200 MA and bounces. Lookback: 4H 60 / D 20 / W 12 / M 6 bars
P3 Small MA Bounce — Bounces off MA 40–100 in uptrend. Dedup window: 3 bars
P4 Small MA Rejection — Rejected at MA 40–100 in downtrend. Dedup window: 3 bars
SEC Secondary — Midpoint bounces, MA cluster touches. Suppressed in NEUTRAL zone.
Confidence & Confluence
high P2 + volume spike + key level = highest conviction
standard Signal + volume spike OR key level confluence
low P3/P4 without volume or key level support
Timeframe Alignment
Triple Bull 4H + D + W + M all uptrend — highest conviction direction
Triple Bear All timeframes downtrend
Counter-trend Signal opposes higher timeframe trend — risky
